while requiring repeated validation attacks. Matched inter-
ventions show that switching to PGD-2 prevents terminal
collapse, whereas rollback contributes no consistent average
improvement and is removed from the final policy. The
resulting model reaches 44.75% PGD-50 accuracy on the full
CIFAR-10 test set and 41.30% standard AutoAttack accuracy
on a fixed 1,000-image subset. These results support the
monitor as a low-cost conditional safeguard, not as a claim
to supersede the stable FGSM-RS reference or full PGD
training. Evaluation on additional architectures and datasets,
and the integration of empirical monitoring with certified
defenses, remain important next steps.
